# Baby Owl Hollow — Design Document

A single-page, screensaver-style scene in the spirit of Sierra's *Johnny Castaway*:
a warm, self-running world with a recurring cast of characters, gentle ambient
motion, and small stories that play out on their own. Bright, cute, storybook colors.

Open `index.html` directly from disk (`file://`) or serve the folder — no build
step, no assets, no network needed.

## Why this implementation

- **SVG + one `requestAnimationFrame` loop.** Crisp vector art at any window size,
  one `viewBox` (1600x900) with `preserveAspectRatio="xMidYMid slice"` so it fills
  the screen like a real screensaver.
- **Classic scripts, not ES modules.** ES modules are blocked by CORS when opened
  via `file://`; plain `<script src>` tags work everywhere. Files share globals.
- **Everything generated by JS.** The owls, flowers, tufts, butterflies etc. are
  built programmatically so poses/animations are just transform updates.
- **No external assets, fonts, or libraries.** System font stack only.

## File layout

| File | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | SVG stage, gradient defs, UI overlay (buttons, hint), script tags |
| `css/style.css` | Fullscreen stage, UI pill styling, cursor-idle behavior |
| `js/scene.js` | Shared helpers (`svgEl`, `cubic`, easing, `mixHex`), background: sky, sun, moon, stars, clouds, hills, ground, tree, pond, grass details, butterflies, leaves, sparkles, fireflies, hedgehog, frog, bees, rain/snow/rainbow; atmosphere clock (day/night, weather, seasons); ambient critters + swing sway (`updateCritters`) |
| `js/owls.js` | `Owl` class: body/wings/eyes/beak/feet built from SVG shapes; per-frame update (blink, gaze tracking, flap, hop, bob, sleepy lids); speech bubbles |
| `js/stories.js` | Story context `S` (wait/skip, fly paths, splash, fish, zzz, hearts, berries, pebbles, constellations, notes) + the 8 stories as async scripts |
| `js/main.js` | Boot, rAF loop, mouse/keyboard/click input, story scheduler (day rotation + once-per-night night story), WebAudio sound, fullscreen |

SVG layer order (bottom → top): `bg` → `mid` (pond, tree) → `fg` (grass details)
→ `owls` → `critters` → `fx` (bubbles, splashes, zzz, rain/snow, huddle leaf)
→ `tint` (day/night overlay) → vignette → `debug` (G-key alignment overlay).
`critters`, `fx`, `tint` and `debug` are click-through.

## The cast

- **Olive** (parent, brown, scale 1.05) — perched on the big branch at (1120, 393).
- **Pip** (baby, light tan, scale 0.62) — on the branch at (950, 419).
- **Puff** (baby, soft grey-blue, scale 0.60) — on a red mushroom at (800, 682).
- **The hedgehog** — a shy little critter (`Scene.hedgehog`, scale 1.3 with a
  ground shadow) that waddles in along the pond's right shore during its story
  and hides the rest of the time.
- **The frog** (`Scene.frog`) — hops between the pond's lily pads and croaks
  "ribbit!" on landing (daytime).
- **The squirrel** (`Scene.squirrel`) — runs back and forth along the branch
  (tree layer, so it passes behind the owls) and stops to chatter at Pip.
- **The bees** (`Scene.bees`) — loop around the beehive (`Scene.hive`) that
  hangs from the tree's side branch; honey drips from it now and then.
- **The swing** (`Scene.swing`) — hangs from the main branch and sways; every
  so often in daytime idle, Pip flies over, rides it for a few swings, and
  flies home (the swing ride lives in `main.js`: `updateSwing`/`swingRideGo`;
  a story or nightfall aborts it via `abortSwing`).

Owls are built from layered shapes (body, scalloped belly, ear tufts, facial discs,
big amber-irised eyes with movable pupils, blush, beak, side wings, feet) so
animation = transform updates:
wings rotate around the shoulder, eyes squash for blinks and droop when sleepy,
pupils track the mouse, the whole body bobs (breathing) and hops.

## The eight stories (rotate automatically, ~12–18 s idle gap)

1. **First Flight** — Pip hops, flaps excitedly, glides from the branch to a stub
   near the trunk while Olive watches, then flies back. Proud hoots, happy wiggles.
2. **Fishing Lesson** — Puff watches from the mushroom as Olive flies to the pond,
   waits by the water, catches a fish (splash + arc into beak), flies back for a
   snack. Then Puff tries, gets a big splash and no fish, and Olive cheers anyway.
3. **Nap Time** — Olive yawns and "tells a story", both babies' eyes droop, `z`
   floats up, a butterfly lands on Olive's head. Gentle wake-up stretch.
4. **Bath Time** — the babies fly into the shallows at the pond's near edge
   (feet in the water) and splash about (hops + splashes at the feet) while
   Olive supervises from the branch.
5. **The New Friend** — a shy hedgehog waddles in along the pond's right shore;
   Pip flies down to the water's edge to greet it, Puff shares a berry, and
   everyone waves goodbye as it waddles off (turning around first).
6. **Lost & Found** — Pip drops a shiny pebble from the branch (gravity fall,
   bounce, glint), Puff searches the grass, spots it, and returns it — Pip is
   delighted, Olive is proud.
7. **Constellation Lesson** *(night story — plays once per night)* — the owls
   look up as the stars draw an owl in the sky, twinkle, and fade; the babies
   gasp, then everyone tucks back in.
8. **Band Practice** — the three owls hoot a little melody (low, mid, high, then
   a chord) with floating ♪ notes and a group bow.

Stories are async functions using `await S.wait(ms)`; **Space** (or clicking the
background) skips the current story — waits reject with a `SKIP` sentinel and the
runner restores everyone to their home pose (and hides the hedgehog). Keys
**1–8** jump to a story. Day stories rotate automatically; the night story plays
once per night when the owls would otherwise sleep.

## Ambient life (always running)

Drifting clouds, rotating sun rays, birds, swaying grass/flowers/reeds, pond
ripples, Lissajous-flying butterflies, occasional falling leaves, floating
sparkles, random idle emotes (hops, wiggles, wing stretches, blinks), a frog
hopping lily pads and croaking, a squirrel scampering the branch and chattering
at Pip, bees looping around the beehive with honey drips, the swing swaying
(and Pip riding it in daytime idle), and shooting stars at night.

## Geometry & alignment (single source of truth)

Alignment bugs — an owl "in" the pond that is really standing on the bank —
come from duplicated magic numbers, so the geometry lives in exactly one place
and everything derives from it:

- `Scene.pond` — the pond ellipse (center (400, 795), rx 285, ry 80).
  `buildMid` **draws** the pond from it; `Scene.pondEdgeY(x, near)` (edge y at
  x), `Scene.inPond(x, y)` and `Scene.pondShallow(x, scale, inset)` (the
  body-center anchor that puts an owl's feet `inset` px inside the near edge)
  derive from it. Bath spots in `POS` are `Scene.pondShallow(...)` values.
- `OWL_FEET` (owls.js) — the local y of an owl's feet/shadow line (74).
  `owl.feetY()` is the world y of the ground contact. An owl's `POS` anchor is
  its body **center**, so feet = `y + OWL_FEET * scale` — always validate the
  feet, not the anchor.
- `Scene.keepClear` — zones props and story spots must avoid (the tree trunk).
- `Scene.safeArea` — the region guaranteed visible on common screens (the
  viewBox minus the 16:10 `slice` side crop), and `Scene.uiZones` — the HTML UI
  zones to keep clear of (the bottom hint pill). Story action must stay inside
  the safe area and out of the UI zones, or it is cut off / washed out.
- `G` — debug overlay (topmost `debug` layer): the pond outline, keep-clear
  zones, safe area + UI zones, every `POS` spot, and the owls' rendered anchors
  + feet lines, labeled and tracking the owls live.

The smoke test enforces the contract: the drawn pond matches `Scene.pond`; bath
spots have anchor AND feet inside the pond with the feet within 30 px of the
near edge (the shallows); every other story spot — and the feet of every owl
spot on land — stays out of the pond; no `POS` spot sits in a keep-clear zone;
every owl spot's feet plus the pebble/hedgehog ground stay inside the safe area
and clear of the UI zones (nothing cut off at the bottom or behind the hint).

## Atmosphere (day/night, weather, seasons)

The world lives on a clock: one full day/night cycle every 3 minutes
(`Scene.dayMs`). `Scene.dayT` (0–1) drives everything:

- **Sky** — gradient stops lerp between day → dusk → night → dawn palettes;
  the sun arcs across the sky (hidden at night), a moon crosses at night,
  stars twinkle, fireflies wander the meadow. Butterflies and sparkles fade
  out after dark.
- **Tint** — a full-stage rect (`#tint`, above `fx`, below the vignette)
  warms the scene at dusk and darkens it at night.
- **Sleep** — at night (`Scene.isNight()`) the owls stay home, eyes droop
  shut, breathing deepens and `z`s float up; the story scheduler (`tryStart`)
  plays the night story (Constellation Lesson) once per night, then waits out
  the rest of the night, re-checking every 8 s. Manual story keys still work —
  the owls wake for the show. Idle emotes pause while asleep or huddled.
- **Weather** — clear → rain (snow in winter) → rainbow (after rain) →
  clear (`Scene.weather`, `nextWeather`). Rain streaks fall in the `fx`
  layer and ripple the pond; a rainbow arcs behind the hills after the rain.
  While it precipitates (and it's not night), the owls huddle together on
  the branch under a big leaf and squint (`updateRest` in `main.js`).
- **Seasons** — one in-world day per season (`Scene.seasonDays`), rotating
  spring → summer → autumn → winter as `dayT` wraps (`Scene.dayCount`).
  Canopy, hills and grass recolor per season; spring grows blossom and sheds
  pink petals, autumn sheds many orange leaves, winter thins the canopy,
  hides the berries and blankets the ground in snow.

## Interaction

- **Mouse move** — pupils/head track the cursor; cursor hides after 3 s idle
  (screensaver behavior).
- **Click an owl** — hop + "Hoo!" bubble + heart.
- **Click the pond** — splash + ripples.
- **Click the background** — skip the current story.
- **Keys** — `1`–`8` story, `Space` skip, `F` fullscreen, `M` sound,
  `G` debug overlay (see "Geometry & alignment").
- **Buttons** — sound toggle (WebAudio, default off), fullscreen.
- Subtle mouse parallax on clouds and hills.

## Sound (tiny, WebAudio, off by default)

Hoot = two descending sine blips; splash = quick square+sine drop; bubble pop =
short triangle blip; wing flap = soft low blip on flight start; frog croak =
low sawtooth croak; bee buzz = quiet square wobble; squirrel chatter = rapid
square ticks.

## Verification

`node --check` on every JS file; serve with `python3 -m http.server` and load in
a browser; headless screenshot if a browser is available.
